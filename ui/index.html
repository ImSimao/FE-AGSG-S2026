Data format

    
CSV per line: x,y,heading[,front,rear,left,right]. Units: meters for x/y/distances, degrees for heading (0 = +x axis, counter-clockwise). Distances are optional and used in Telemetry mode.

    
Examples: 0.5,1.2,45 or 0.5,1.2,45,0.30,0.25,0.40,0.35
